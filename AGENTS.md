# Working rules

Read docs/FOUNDATION.md and docs/FOUNDING-CONVERSATION-AR.md first. The foundation is the canonical starting point; the Arabic founding conversation preserves the user's intent, corrections, rejected approaches, and distinction between agreed principles and proposals. Read docs/design/01-birth.md for the current design work. Do not treat historical sketches or unapproved proposals as specifications.

- Advance the single actual product, not a collection of demos, experiments, or alternative products.
- Do not import old UI, code, design systems, names, layouts, prompts, or assumptions from other branches as product requirements.
- Keep the user's experience coherent and continuous; do not equate one world with one inflexible screen.
- Technologies are selected for a demonstrated product purpose, accessibility, performance, security, and maintainability. Novelty is not a requirement.
- Do not fake discoveries, citations, tool results, completed actions, or certainty. Distinguish observed facts from generated proposals.
- Do not introduce visible Matter/Void taxonomies, tender feeds, dashboards, onboarding questionnaires, or abstract decorative interfaces by default.
- Do not implement undecided design details or deploy without the user's direction. Record decisions and open questions in the product's own evolving documentation.
- Never modify main, existing deployments, or existing databases as part of this branch without explicit direction.
- Each change should advance the actual product and carry verifiable acceptance criteria.
