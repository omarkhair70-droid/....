# Opportunity OS — Foundation
Date: 2026-11-14
Status: initial product design; principles agreed in conversation, detailed first-state behavior proposed for refinement.

## 1. Constitution

The product is a continuous digital world in which people create commercial possibilities from real capabilities, relationships, circumstances and timing. Its identity persists through discovery, thought, creation, decisions and execution. It is not a tender feed, a conventional dashboard, a chatbot, or a set of disconnected experiences.

The human should understand the result without needing to understand the complexity that produced it. Nature inspires the processes of formation, timing, context, development, adaptation and continuity, not a compulsory organic aesthetic or a literal biological simulation. Mathematics, physics, cinema, music, architecture, interaction design, AI, and computer graphics are available as instruments when they serve a concrete purpose.

One product means one evolving canonical experience, not thousands of variants. It does not prohibit safe engineering tests or code review. No prior visual language is binding. Matter and Void are not visible product concepts. Relationships must have an evidential basis; a commercial possibility is not a guarantee or an established fact.

## 2. First state — before any interaction

The product's first act is to establish the human's real context, with minimal effort and without inventing an identity or opportunity.

A world already known to the product resumes from its last meaningful state. New evidence may alter it without destroying the user's decisions or history. An unknown world begins without pretending to know the user; identity or context can be supplied through a single intelligible action. No compulsory survey, example-company data masquerading as reality, or artificial welcome animation.

Proposed semantic contract (not screen copy or visual layout):
- First visit, no authorized context: identify the person/company or provide a real starting point, with an equally clear path to skip.
- Sufficient authorized context: surface one meaningful, actionable relationship that can be explained through specific facts, if one exists.
- Insufficient evidence: continue investigation only where permitted, communicate the limitation plainly when relevant, and let the human correct or supply context. Never fabricate value to fill the interface.
- Return visit: restore ongoing work, preserve decisions, and reveal a meaningful change only when one exists.

The intent is to make the first meaningful action understandable without requiring knowledge of the underlying algorithms. It must be immediately possible to stop, correct an inference, decline a connection, or resume work.

## 3. Continuity model

Identity, evidence (source, date, provenance), relationships (basis and constraints), proposals (assumptions and uncertainty), human decisions, and actions (authorization and outcome) are persistent concepts underneath the experience, not mandatory visible modules. A relationship can be verified without implying that its associated proposed business will succeed. Time and circumstance are first-class dimensions. New evidence can change a proposal while its history remains inspectable.

Changes in representation must correspond to meaningful changes in the underlying state, not decoration. The world must remain usable without motion or audio. It must not require understanding any scientific metaphor.

## 4. First-state acceptance criteria

1. A new user understands what can be done without learning a special vocabulary.
2. A returning user retains their context and decisions.
3. Every presented real-world relationship has traceable evidence; unsupported proposals are not treated as established facts.
4. Missing access, network failure, or incomplete evidence never produces invented results.
5. There is a clear, reversible next action; external actions require appropriate authorization.
6. Keyboard access, reduced motion, readable text, and responsive behavior are designed from the start.
7. The first state is part of the final product, not a disposable demo.

## 5. Next design decision

Define the actual first meaningful encounter in the single world: what the human perceives, what the product does, what the human can do next, and exactly how each state transitions. Specify visual composition, timing, inputs, hierarchy, copy, accessibility and failure behavior together before coding that encounter. Do not choose a rendering technology, palette, metaphor, name, or page hierarchy in advance of this decision.

No front-end, back-end, deployment, or visual implementation has been created or approved by this document.
