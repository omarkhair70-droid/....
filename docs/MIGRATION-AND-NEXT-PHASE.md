## Next phase — synthesis, search and invention
The founder wants intensive synthesis before selecting the actual stack. Research broadly across computational science, mathematics, interaction design, rendering, distributed systems, AI, privacy and human collaboration. Compare established systems and prior art. Build a traceable map from each genuine product requirement to possible methods, evidence, compatibility, performance, cost and trade-offs. Identify gaps where custom instruments, languages, algorithms or runtimes could earn their place; combine existing tools where adequate. Distinguish a candidate from a selected component; distinguish an innovation hypothesis from verified novelty. Decide architecture and visual expression from the requirements of the single world, not from an arbitrary collection of libraries.

No application implementation, benchmarks, live integrations or deployment are claimed by this import. Do not import rejected designs or old product assumptions. Build one actual product, with appropriate engineering tests and human verification, rather than disposable competing interfaces.
