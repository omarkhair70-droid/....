# 26 — FORMATION A / لحظة ولادة الفكرة
15 نوفمبر 2026. انتقال إلى السلايس الثانية بطلب المؤسس، بعد نجاح World web وWorld kernel في 0a0749f. لا يعني إغلاق التقييم الفني لـ EXISTENCE أو تحقق التسجيل والتفريغ على عتاد حقيقي. مرجع: الستوري بورد 25–29، ووثائق 10 (اتجاه الخزف المرفوض)، 11 و24.
## السؤال
هل يستطيع الإنسان أن يمسك جزءًا من تفكيره، يعيد تأليفه، ويمنحه وجودًا مستقلًا قبل اختيار مخاطَب؟ لا نرسم فكرة على أنها كرة أو كيان تجاري مزيف. المادة هي الكلام نفسه؛ الأصل لحظة محددة، والفرع كتابة جديدة.
## التنفيذ
Native textarea selection supplies actual UTF-16 range or the whole text when no range is selected. Whitespace selection is rejected; no hidden truncation or rewriting. Capture immutable excerpt without copying unrelated content; compose in a distinct authored surface with source and new wording, cancel or explicitly keep a separate local work with its own id and parent plus origin excerpt. Parent remains unchanged. The original remains active; entering a child is explicit and honors the existing voice-clear confirmation. Children and origins appear in the private field; returning does not mutate the source. Stored v1 records remain compatible and optional origin is validated. No implicit persistence or network call. Recipient view clears private trace text; this is not an authenticated boundary. Existing casting path still available after forming; Formation is not a claim of integrated Rust persistence.
## Verification and limits
Module and browser tests cover selected range, independent edits, locale, retained trace, explicit persistence, restore and scoped rendering. No AI interpretation, no claim of authorship verification, lawful attribution, multi-user collaboration or backend integration. Voice recordings remain temporary; accepted text can be selected like other text, not linked to word-level audio evidence. Artistic composition, keyboard/IME/zoom, real audio and human review remain open. No merge or production promotion.
